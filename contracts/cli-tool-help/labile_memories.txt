chitta labile_memories - List memories recalled multiple times recently — candidates for reconsolidation (excludes freshly-written hook memories)

Parameters:
  --limit
      Max results (default 20)
  --min_access
      Min recall count to qualify (default 2)
  --realm
      Filter by realm
  --window_hours
      Recency window in hours (default 48)

Example:
  chitta labile_memories
