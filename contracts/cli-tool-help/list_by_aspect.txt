chitta list_by_aspect - List memories by semantic aspect

Parameters:
  --aspect (required)
      Aspect: preferences|corrections|insights|failures|decisions|approaches|milestones|goals|habits|beliefs|wisdom|code|gaps
  --limit [default: 50]
      Max results
  --min_confidence [default: 0.1]
      Minimum confidence

Example:
  chitta list_by_aspect --aspect "..."
