chitta assert_fact - Assert a constraint fact (subject-predicate-object) with provenance and scope. Auto-detects conflicts and creates rival branches.

Parameters:
  --branch_id
      Branch to assert into (0=trunk)
  --confidence
      Confidence 0-1 (default 0.8)
  --confidence_basis
      Basis: stated, observed, derived, corrected
  --object (required)
      Value (e.g. 'Rust', 'vim', 'Copenhagen')
  --predicate (required)
      Relation (e.g. 'prefers', 'uses', 'located-in')
  --provenance_source
      Source: user, tool, distillation, inference
  --scope
      Scope: global, realm name, or session (default: global)
  --subject (required)
      Entity (e.g. 'user', 'project-X')

Example:
  chitta assert_fact --subject "..." --predicate "..." --object "..."
