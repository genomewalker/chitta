chitta export_training_pairs - Export query-passage pairs as JSONL for embedding fine-tuning

Parameters:
  --output_path
      Output JSONL path
  --realm
      Filter to specific realm
  --max_pairs [default: 10000]
      Max pairs to export
  --min_confidence [default: 0.5]
      Min confidence threshold
  --include_negatives [default: true]
      Generate hard negatives

Example:
  chitta export_training_pairs
