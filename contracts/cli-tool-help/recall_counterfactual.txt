chitta recall_counterfactual - CDAWG sibling-edge counterfactual: what alternative tool/entity would have had a lower failure rate in this same context?

Parameters:
  --entity (required)

  --k

  --outcome
      0=success 1=fail 2=error 3=partial (default 1)
  --tool (required)


Example:
  chitta recall_counterfactual --tool "..." --entity "..."
