chitta grow - Add wisdom, belief, failure, aspiration, or dream

Parameters:
  --type (required)
      Type: wisdom|belief|failure|aspiration|dream
  --content (required)
      Content to store
  --title
      Short title
  --tags
      Comma-separated tags
  --realm
      Primary realm (default: brahman)
  --visibility [default: 0]
      0=Private, 1=Shared, 2=Global

Example:
  chitta grow --type "..." --content "..."
