chitta span_query - Retrieve verbatim transcript atoms (paths, URLs, file:line, bash commands, errors). Exact-substring, no LLM/GPU, realm-scoped.

Parameters:
  --query (required)
      Substring/token to match
  --realm
      Restrict to realm (empty = unscoped)
  --k [default: 6]
      Max atoms

Example:
  chitta span_query --query "..."
