chitta trigger_add - Create a trigger automaton (prospective memory). Arms on creation, fires when conditions met or tension exceeds threshold.

Parameters:
  --action (required)
      Action on fire (Notify, InjectMemory, EmitEvent, RememberFact)
  --condition (required)
      Trigger condition (TimeAfter, ConstraintMatch, EventMatch, AllOf, AnyOf)
  --deadline_ms
      Deadline timestamp ms (0=no deadline)
  --gain
      Emotional importance 0-1 (default 0.5)
  --name (required)
      Human-readable trigger name
  --realm
      Realm scope (default: global)
  --tension_threshold
      Tension level to auto-fire (default 0.8)

Example:
  chitta trigger_add --name "..." --condition "..." --action "..."
