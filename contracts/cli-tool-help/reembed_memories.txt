chitta reembed_memories - Re-embed memories with missing/zero embeddings

Parameters:
  --all [default: false]
      Re-embed ALL memories with NULL embeddings
  --limit [default: 100]
      Max memories to process
  --kind
      Filter by kind: belief, wisdom, episode, correction, preference
  --min_confidence [default: 0]
      Min confidence threshold
  --dry_run [default: false]
      Preview without updating

Example:
  chitta reembed_memories
