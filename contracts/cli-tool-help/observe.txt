chitta observe - Store observation (used by hooks for [LEARN] extraction)

Parameters:
  --title (required)
      Short title
  --content (required)
      Full content
  --category [default: episode]
      Category: wisdom|insight|signal|episode
  --tags
      Comma-separated tags
  --realm
      Primary realm (default: brahman)
  --source [default: mcp_tool]
      Source: hook_regex|hook_compliance|distillation|mcp_tool
  --confidence
      Override confidence (0.0-1.0)
  --evidence
      What triggered this observation
  --force_supersede_ids
      Comma-separated memory IDs to explicitly supersede

Example:
  chitta observe --title "..." --content "..."
