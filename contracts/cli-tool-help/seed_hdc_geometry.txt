chitta seed_hdc_geometry - CEC Phase 17 Part D: seed HDC codebook from vocab_geometry harvest JSON. Binarizes f32 PCA directions from open-weight embedding matrix into HdcVec entries, replacing random hash projections for harvested tokens.

Parameters:
  --json_path (required)
      Path to qwen2.5-7b_vocab_geometry.json produced by harvest_ow.py --mode vocab_geometry

Example:
  chitta seed_hdc_geometry --json_path "..."
