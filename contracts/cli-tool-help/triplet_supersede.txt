chitta triplet_supersede - Mark one triplet as superseded by another (bi-temporal update)

Parameters:
  --at_ms
      Ingestion-time of supersession (default: now)
  --new_id (required)
      Replacing triplet ID
  --old_id (required)
      Triplet ID being superseded

Example:
  chitta triplet_supersede --old_id "..." --new_id "..."
