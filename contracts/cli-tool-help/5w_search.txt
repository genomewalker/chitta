chitta 5w_search - Multi-dimensional semantic search across who/what/when/where/why axes

Parameters:
  --limit
      Max results (default 10)
  --realm
      Filter by realm
  --what
      What is happening/topic
  --when
      Temporal description
  --where
      Location or context
  --who
      Who is involved
  --why
      Motivation or reason

Example:
  chitta 5w_search
