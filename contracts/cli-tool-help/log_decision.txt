chitta log_decision - Log a decision point to the DecisionTape: chosen action + alternatives considered and rejected. Enables recall_true_counterfactual.

Parameters:
  --chosen_entity (required)

  --chosen_outcome
      0=success 1=fail 2=error 3=partial
  --chosen_tool (required)

  --confidence_delta
      chosen_confidence - best_alternative_confidence
  --rejected_json
      JSON array of [sym_u64, rejection_reason_u8] pairs
  --ts_ms


Example:
  chitta log_decision --chosen_tool "..." --chosen_entity "..."
