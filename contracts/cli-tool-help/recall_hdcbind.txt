chitta recall_hdcbind - Heteroassociative HDC query: given known_role=known_val, infer query_role. Roles: tool, entity, outcome.

Parameters:
  --known_role (required)
      Known role (tool|entity|outcome)
  --known_val (required)
      Value for the known role
  --query_role (required)
      Role to infer (tool|entity|outcome)
  --k [default: 5]
      Max results

Example:
  chitta recall_hdcbind --known_role "..." --known_val "..." --query_role "..."
