Visualization modes

GALAXY

Force-directed map of all 512 semantic directions. Click any node to enter Radial mode and orbit its neighbors by similarity. Drag, zoom, filter.

RADIAL

A clicked direction moves to center. All others orbit at a distance proportional to 1 − Jaccard — semantically identical directions appear closest. Click empty space or ← to return.

TOKEN ↔ DIRECTION

Bipartite graph: top tokens on the left, directions on the right. Hover a token to highlight every direction that carries it. Hover a direction to reveal its vocabulary.

The probe bar at the bottom is always active. Type a concept and every matching direction lights up across all three modes. The 5×5-pixel strip is the actual binary hypervector fingerprint — each cell is one direction; blue = activated, dim = silent. Hamming distance between two such strips is what chitta uses for recall.
